## Finance Club at the University of Zurich

**The Finance Club** is a student organization with over 2,000 members. It serves as a **platform for exchange, information, and networking** between students, professors, and professionals from the financial industry.

Regular **events such as workshops, Brown Bag Lunches, and networking events** are organized to enable direct exchange with industry experts. This is designed to facilitate students' access to the financial world and prepare them specifically for a career in this field.

The organization is managed by the **Executive Board**, supported by ambassadors and an Advisory Board that provides strategic advice.

Further information and contact details can be found directly at [Finance Club UZH](https://www.financeclub.ch/about-us).
