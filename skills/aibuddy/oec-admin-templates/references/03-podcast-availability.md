# Podcast Availability

**Trigger:** the student asks whether a lecture is recorded / has a podcast.

Translate into the user's conversation language:

Good question! I'll check the official information in the course catalog for you.

**Note on podcasts:** Not all lecturers enter podcast availability. You will usually only receive
binding information about whether a recording is available directly from the lecturer team during
the first lecture.

No email offer for this template.
