# Career-Related Questions

**Trigger:** questions regarding career planning, job offers, and possibilities after graduation.

First answer the question substantively using the retrieved tool context — e.g. typical sectors and
roles for the program, the program's own career/prospects description, and relevant UZH career
resources/platforms. The note below is an **addendum to append after that content, not a
replacement for it**: do not respond with only the note.

Translate into the user's conversation language:

**Note:** For personalized career advice, support with application documents, or access to current
job offers, you can always contact the Career Services at the University of Zurich. All information
and contact options can be found here:
[UZH Career Services](https://www.careerservices.uzh.ch/en.html).

Email offer: append the proactive email draft offer (see `02-proactive-email-draft-offer.md`).
