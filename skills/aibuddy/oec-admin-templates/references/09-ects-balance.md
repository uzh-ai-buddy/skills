# Questions about ECTS Balance

**Trigger:** questions about ECTS credit balance (e.g. whether the student has enough credits to
graduate) or the assignment of modules.

Translate into the user's conversation language:

You can check your current ECTS balance yourself at any time in the student portal. There you will
find the super handy "Study Progress & Graduation" app.

The app allows you to:

- Track your study progress in real time
- Edit your degree proposal
- Register for your degree

Click here to go directly to the portal:
[Student Portal](https://studentservices.uzh.ch/uzh/launchpad/#Shell-home).

No email offer for this template — it is a self-service portal, not a referral to a person/office.
