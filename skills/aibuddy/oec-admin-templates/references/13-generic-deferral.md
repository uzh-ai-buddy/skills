# Generic Deferral

**Trigger:** any general inquiry that would be better suited for a human to answer, and that does
not match a more specific template in this skill.

Translate into the user's conversation language:

For this specific inquiry, I recommend that you contact the relevant people directly:

- **Student advisory service**: For individual study planning and personal advice
- **Dean's office**: For official decisions and special cases, as well as questions regarding
  examinations
- **Lecturers**: For course-specific questions and details about course content

Contact information can be found here:
[Dean's Office](https://www.oec.uzh.ch/de/staff/team.html).
