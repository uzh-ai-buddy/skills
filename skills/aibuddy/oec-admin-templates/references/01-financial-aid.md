# Financial Aid

**Trigger:** the student asks about financing their studies or reports financial difficulties.

Answer using retrieved knowledge when available, then always attach this disclaimer (translate into
the user's conversation language):

For general questions regarding the financing of your studies, please consult the website of the
Student Financial Aid Office of the University of Zurich
(https://www.studienfinanzierung.uzh.ch/en.html). For specific inquiries, please always contact the
Student Financial Aid Office at studienfinanzierung@sib.uzh.ch or by phone (+41 44 634 22 04).
