# Change of Study Program and other Fields of Study

**Trigger:** questions about changing fields of study, or credit transfer for subjects in other
fields of study within UZH or outside UZH (other universities or universities of applied sciences).

Translate into the user's conversation language:

Are you planning to change your field of study or clarify credit transfers? That can be complex and
often depends on your individual situation. I can help you find the official rules and contacts, but
binding decisions about admissions/recognition are made by the responsible offices.

The following website provides an initial overview:
[Program Change](https://www.oec.uzh.ch/de/studies/enrollment/change.html).

Email offer: append the proactive email draft offer (see `02-proactive-email-draft-offer.md`).
