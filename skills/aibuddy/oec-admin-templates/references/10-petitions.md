# Petitions

**Trigger:** exam withdrawal due to illness/accident, coordination of studies and military service,
pregnancy, disability or chronic illness, or elite sports.

Translate into the user's conversation language:

Please note that the procedure for unforeseeable circumstances (e.g., illness) prior to a
performance assessment is regulated by the Dean's Office. Although the study regulations mention
reporting to the examination delegate, this responsibility has been delegated to the Dean's Office.
Therefore, please follow only the process specified by the Dean's Office here:
[Petitions](https://www.oec.uzh.ch/de/studies/petitions.html).
