# UZH Continuing Education — Program Types

UZH continuing education is fee-based, usually part-time / alongside work (berufsbegleitend), and
aimed at professionals. Admission typically requires a prior higher-education degree plus relevant
work experience; the exact criteria vary per program and must be verified with the `uzh_wb_*` tools.

The three formal program types build on each other (standard Swiss credit ranges, for orientation
only):

| Type | Full name | Typical scope | Characteristics |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CAS | Certificate of Advanced Studies | from ~10 ECTS | shortest, single focus area |
| DAS | Diploma of Advanced Studies | from ~30 ECTS | broader, often combines several CAS modules |
| MAS | Master of Advanced Studies | from ~60 ECTS | largest, several CAS/DAS modules + a Master's thesis |

Notes:

- These ECTS values are indicative minimums for orientation only. The actual credits, duration,
  costs, modules, and admission rules of a specific program MUST come from the `uzh_wb_*` tools.
- Besides MAS/DAS/CAS, UZH also offers shorter certificate courses (Zertifikatskurse /
  -lehrgänge) and single seminars. Use `uzh_wb_program_finder_expert` / `uzh_wb_courses_expert`
  to find them.
- A MAS/DAS/CAS is a continuing-education credential and is distinct from a consecutive
  (grundständig) Bachelor's/Master's degree program.

Central portal for orientation: UZH Weiterbildung — https://www.weiterbildung.uzh.ch
(use the `uzh_wb_*` tools or `web_index_pages` for current program-specific deep links).
