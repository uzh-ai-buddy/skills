# OEC BA Business & Economics (BAOEC)

## Programs covered

- Bachelor of Arts UZH in Business and Economics (RVO22)
  - DE: Bachelor of Arts UZH in Wirtschaftswissenschaften (RVO22)
  - Majors (150 ECTS each): Business Administration (DBA), Banking and Finance (DF), Economics
    (ECON)

Internal ID (for reference only; never show to students): BAOEC.

## Assessment year requirement

- The assessment year is mandatory.
- Before booking further courses, 45 ECTS of the assessment year must be completed.
- Use `references/oec-ba-assessment.md` for rules and the full course list.

## Compulsory in BAOEC (after assessment year)

- Courses:
  - `Mikroökonomik II` (3 ECTS, HS, ECON)
  - `BWL III` (6 ECTS, HS, DBA)
  - `Makroökonomik II` (3 ECTS, FS, ECON)
  - `Managerial Accounting` (3 ECTS, FS, DBA)
  - `Financial Reporting` (3 ECTS, FS, DBA)
  - `Einführung in die empirische Wirtschaftsforschung` (6 ECTS, HS, ECON)
  - `Einführung in das wissenschaftliche Arbeiten` (3 ECTS, HS, ECON)
- `Bachelor's thesis` (18 ECTS)

## Compulsory courses BAOEC — Banking and Finance major

- `Corporate Finance` (6 ECTS, HS, DF)
- `Banking` (6 ECTS, HS, DF)
- `Asset Pricing` (6 ECTS, FS, DF)
- `Financial Economics` (6 ECTS, FS, DF)

## Compulsory courses BAOEC — Business Administration (DBA) major

- No compulsory courses
- Requirement to complete at least 3 ECTS credits per area BWL 1 to BWL 6

## Compulsory courses BAOEC — Economics (ECON) major

- No compulsory courses
- Requirement to complete at least 12 ECTS credits per area ECON 1 and ECON 2
