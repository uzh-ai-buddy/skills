# OEC MA Business & Economics (MAOEC)

## Programs covered

- Master of Arts UZH in Business and Economics (RVO22)
  - DE: Master of Arts UZH in Wirtschaftswissenschaften (RVO22)
  - Majors (90 ECTS each): Banking and Finance (DF), Business Administration (DBA), Economics
    (ECON), Management and Economics (DBA)

Internal ID (for reference only; never show to students): MAOEC.

## Compulsory in MAOEC

- No mandatory courses for all MAOEC, mandatory modules depend on major.
- `Master's thesis` (30 ECTS)

## Compulsory courses MAOEC — Economics major (`M_90_ECON`):

- `Advanced Macroeconomics` (6 ECTS, HS, ECON)
- `Advanced Microeconomics I` (6 ECTS, HS, ECON)
- `Advanced Microeconomics II` (6 ECTS, FS, ECON)
- `Empirical Methods` (6 ECTS, FS, ECON)
- `International Macroeconomics` (6 ECTS, FS, ECON)
- Requirement to complete at least 24 ECTS credits from the Economic area (ECON 1 - ECON 3)

## Compulsory courses MAOEC — Business Administration major (`M_90_BWL`):

- `Applied Empirical Methods for Business Administration`
  (6 ECTS, FS, DBA)
- `Fortgeschrittene Mikroökonomik für BWL` (6 ECTS, FS, DBA)
- Requirement to complete at least 48 ECTS credits from the Business
  Administration area (BWL 1 – BWL 6)

## Compulsory courses MAOEC — Banking and Finance major (`M_90_BF`):

- `Advanced Banking` (6 ECTS, FS, DF)
- `Advanced Corporate Finance I` (6 ECTS, HS, DF)
- `Advanced Financial Economics` (6 ECTS, FS, DF)
- `Empirical Methods` (6 ECTS, HS, ECON)
- `Quantitative Finance` (6 ECTS, FS, DF)
- Requirement to complete at least 18 ECTS credits from the Banking and Finance area (BF 1 – BF 5)

## Compulsory courses MAOEC — Management and Economics (`M_90_ME`):

- `Corporate Finance` (3 ECTS, HS, DF)
- `Advanced Microeconomics I` (6 ECTS, HS, ECON)
- `Empirical Methods` (6 ECTS, HS, ECON)
- `ME 1 Personnel Economics` (6 ECTS, HS, DBA)
- `ME 2 The Economics of Innovation` (6 ECTS, FS, DBA)
- `ME 3 Organizational Economics` (6 ECTS, FS, DBA)
- `ME 4 Accounting & Economics` (3 ECTS, FS, DBA)
- Requirement to complete at least 6 ECTS credits from the Business
  Administration area (BWL 1 – BWL 6) and at least 6 ECTS credits from the Economic area (ECON 1 - ECON 3)
