# OEC BA Assessment Year (BAOEC + BScINF)

## Assessment year rules

- Fixed mandatory modules across two semesters (30 ECTS HS + 30 ECTS FS).
- The mandatory modules of the assessment cannot be replaced by other similar modules outside the faculty.
- All required assessment modules must be completed within four consecutive semesters
  (assessment deadline).
- Before booking further courses, 45 ECTS of the assessment year must be completed.
- Courses not part of the assessment for BScINF can only be booked after 45 ECTS are completed
  (and vice versa for BAOEC).
- If any assessment-year course is failed twice, students may not continue in any study program
  of Economics and Business Administration including Informatics (BScINF, BAOEC).
- The assessment year can only be started in the fall semester (HS).
- The assessment level is an integral part of the Bachelor’s program (BAOEC and BScINF).

## Assessment year courses (common to BAOEC + BScINF)

- `Mathematik I` (6 ECTS, HS, DBA)
- `Mathematik II` (6 ECTS, FS, DBA)
- `Betriebswirtschaftslehre I` (3 ECTS, HS, DBA)
- `Betriebswirtschaftslehre II` (3 ECTS, FS, DBA)
- `Informatik und Wirtschaft` (3 ECTS, HS, IFI)
