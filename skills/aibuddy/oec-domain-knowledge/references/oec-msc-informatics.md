# OEC MA Informatics (MScINF)

## Program covered

- EN: Master of Science UZH in Informatics (RVO22)
- DE: Master of Science UZH in Informatik (RVO22)

Internal ID (for reference only; never show to students): MScINF.

## Majors

- Artificial Intelligence
- Data Science
- People-Oriented Computing
- Information Systems
- Software Systems

## Compulsory in MScINF

- No mandatory courses for all MScINF, mandatory modules depend on major.
- `Master's project` (15 ECTS; information on booking: A3)
- `Master's thesis` (30 ECTS)

## Compulsory courses MScINF - Information Systems (`M_90_IS`):

- `Information Management` (6 ECTS, HS, IFI)
- Requirement to complete 18 ECTS credits from the INF IS area

## Compulsory courses MScINF - Software Systems (`M_90_SOSY`):

- `Fundamentals of Software Systems` (6 ECTS, HS, IFI)
- Requirement to complete 18 ECTS credits from the INF SOSY area

## Compulsory courses MScINF - People-Oriented Computing (`M_90_POC`):

- `Fundamentals of People-Oriented Computing` (6 ECTS, HS, IFI)
- Requirement to complete 18 ECTS credits from the INF POC area

## Compulsory courses MScINF - Artificial Intelligence (`M_90_AI`):

- `Advanced Topics in Artificial Intelligence` (6 ECTS, HS, IFI)
- Requirement to complete 18 ECTS credits from the INF AI area

## Compulsory courses MScINF - Data Science (`M_90_DS`):

- `Foundations of Data Science`(6 ECTS, HS, IFI)
- Requirement to complete 18 ECTS credits from the INF DS area
