# OEC BSc Informatics (BScINF)

## Program covered:

- EN: Bachelor of Science UZH in Informatics (RVO22)
- DE: Bachelor of Science UZH in Informatik (RVO22)

Internal ID (for reference only; never show to students): BScINF.

## Majors

- Software Systems (150 ECTS)
- Information Systems (150 ECTS)
- Informatics with Natural Sciences (120 ECTS)

## Assessment year requirement

- The assessment year is mandatory.
- Before booking further courses, 45 ECTS of the assessment year must be completed.
- Use `references/oec-ba-assessment.md` for rules and the full course list.

## Mandatory BScin INF (after assessment year)

- Courses:
  - `Software Construction` (6 ECTS, HS, IFI)
  - `Software Engineering` (3 ECTS, FS, IFI)
  - `Software Engineering Lab` (6 ECTS, FS, IFI)
  - `Database Systems` (6 ECTS, FS, IFI)
  - `Informatik-Praktikum` (3 ECTS, any term, IFI)
  - `Einführung in das wissenschaftliche Arbeiten` (3 ECTS, HS, ECON)
- `Bachelor's thesis` (18 ECTS)
